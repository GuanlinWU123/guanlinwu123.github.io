General

I am currently a Master student at Johns Hopkins University. My recent research focus on 3D Human Pose Estimation, Computer Vision in Transportation and Embodied AI. I received my Bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Engineering from The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen (CUHK-Shenzhen). During my undergraduate years, I am fortunate to have been mentored by Prof. Changhong Lu and Prof. Yutao He. Previously, I have done several research projects including 3D Human Pose Estimation, Robot Learning and Approximation Algorithms.

I'm actively looking for PhD positions enrolled in 2026 Spring/Fall and open for any kind of collaboration. If you are interested in my research, please contact me through email.
